### Licensing and Security

Spino holds Gaming Licence 0000031 issued by the Tobique Gaming Commission. The commission is the regulator of the Tobique First Nation in New Brunswick, Canada, and licenses online gaming operators under an indigenous-nation gaming framework. Tobique-licensed sites are required to keep player deposits in segregated bank accounts away from operational funds, which protects player balances in the event of operator insolvency.

In practical terms, a Tobique license sits in the offshore tier alongside the new Curacao GCB and Anjouan frameworks. It is a notch below Malta or the UK Gambling Commission for binding dispute resolution, though the fund segregation requirement is genuinely useful. Player recourse runs through the regulator rather than through a strong consumer-court system.
