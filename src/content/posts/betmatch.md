### Licensing and Security

BetMatch holds an active Curacao license, 8048/JAZ2020-013, under Antillephone N.V. That license is verifiable and current, which is the baseline you want. The complication is that some reports also mention the Kahnawake Gaming Commission as the regulator, and the two do not agree. When the regulatory body itself is reported inconsistently, player recourse becomes harder to map, which matters if a dispute ever lands.

The ownership picture is the bigger flag. Across sources, the operator is named as Firo Lod N.V., Smart Solutions LLC, or Fivebet S.r.l., with no single, publicly disclosed corporate structure tying them together. For a 2023 brand this is unusual. Established operators do not usually have three different names attached to them. KYC is mandatory before withdrawals. Expect to submit a valid ID, such as a passport, national ID, or driver's license, plus a utility bill for address verification.
