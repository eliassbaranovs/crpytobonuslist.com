### Licensing and Security

Great Slots holds an Anjouan license under reference ALSI-202508057-FI2. The Anjouan offshore framework, run from the Autonomous Island of Anjouan in the Union of the Comoros, started issuing direct licenses to operators in 2023 and gained traction in 2024 and 2025 as Curacao retired its master-license model.

In practical terms, an Anjouan license sits in the same tier as the new Curacao GCB framework. It is a tier below Malta, the Isle of Man, and the UK Gambling Commission. Player protection depends heavily on the operator behaving well, since the regulator does not run an independent dispute service that delivers binding decisions on complaints.

Brightsky Limited, the operating company, lists a Belize registration. Softgate Group, the payment partner, is a Cypriot entity. These are common offshore structures and pose no red flag on their own, but they signal that any legal escalation would route through commercial litigation rather than a strong regulator.

Site security uses standard SSL encryption. The login flow routes through Keycloak, an open-source identity broker maintained by Red Hat, which is a slightly more modern stack than the bespoke authentication most casinos run.
